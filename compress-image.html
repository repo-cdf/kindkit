How to compress images

  1. Choose one or more images.
  2. Pick a quality and, if you like, a maximum size.
  3. Click “Compress images” and download them, or all as a ZIP.

Also known as: reduce image size, compress JPG, compress PNG, compress photo, image compressor, make image smaller, reduce photo file size, image optimizer.

Questions & answers

How much smaller will my images get?

Photos often shrink by 60–90%, especially from phones and cameras.

Can I compress many images at once?

Yes. Add as many as you like and download them all as one ZIP file.

Why did my PNG become WebP?

“Auto” turns PNGs into WebP, which is much smaller and keeps transparency. Pick PNG if you need that format.
