Questions & answers

How do I convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG?

Add your HEIC photos, choose JPG and click Convert. It works on your phone too.

What happens to transparent backgrounds in JPG?

JPG can’t store transparency, so transparent areas become white. Choose PNG or WebP to keep it.

Which format makes the smallest files?

WebP is usually the smallest, followed by JPG. PNG is the largest but lossless.
