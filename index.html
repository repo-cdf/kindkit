Questions & answers

Is Kindkit really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no limits, no watermarks and no sign-up. There is no paid version. Kindkit is supported by a few ads and by kind people who choose to donate.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. All the work happens on your own device, inside your browser. Your files are never sent to a server, so we can't see them, store them or share them.

Is there a file size limit?

There's no fixed limit. Because everything runs on your device, very large files (hundreds of megabytes) depend on how much memory your phone or computer has. If something is slow, try fewer files at a time.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Kindkit works in any modern browser on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Linux. You can even turn iPhone HEIC photos into JPGs or PDFs.

Do the QR codes expire?

Never. Kindkit makes static QR codes that contain your link or text directly. There's no redirect service in between, so they keep working forever and don't track anyone.

How can I help?

Share Kindkit with someone who might need it, or leave a small tip if you'd like to. Both keep the tools free for everyone.
