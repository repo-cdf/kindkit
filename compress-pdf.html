How to reduce a PDF’s file size

  1. Choose the PDF you want to make smaller.
  2. Pick a level: Smallest file, Balanced, or Best quality.
  3. Click “Compress PDF” and download the smaller file.

Also known as: reduce PDF size, shrink PDF, make PDF smaller, PDF compressor, compress PDF for email, compress scanned PDF, PDF size reducer.

Questions & answers

How much smaller will my PDF get?

Scanned and photo-heavy PDFs often shrink by 50–90%. Text-only PDFs are usually small already.

Will the text still be selectable?

No. Pages are rebuilt as images to save space, so this tool works best for scans and photos.

What if my PDF is already small?

Kindkit checks the result. If compressing wouldn’t help, it tells you and keeps your original.
