Questions & answers

Do these QR codes expire?

Never. They’re static codes that contain your link directly, with no redirect service that could stop working or charge you later.

Can I make a Wi-Fi QR code?

Yes. Guests scan it and join your Wi-Fi without typing the password.

Can I use the QR code for my business or print it?

Yes, free for any use. Download the SVG for sharp printing at any size.
