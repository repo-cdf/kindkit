Combine several PDF files into one document, in the order you choose.

Your files stay on your device and are never uploaded

How to merge PDF files

  1. Choose the PDF files you want to combine, or drag them onto the box.
  2. Put them in the right order using the arrows, or by dragging them.
  3. Click “Merge PDFs” and download your new combined PDF.

Also known as: combine PDF, join PDF files, PDF merger, merge PDF online free, combine PDF files into one, merge PDF without uploading, merge PDF no watermark.

Questions & answers

Is it safe to merge private PDFs here?

Yes. Your PDFs are merged by your own device, inside your browser. They are never uploaded to a server, so nobody else can see them.

How many PDFs can I merge at once?

As many as you like. There are no daily limits or file caps. Very large files simply depend on how much memory your device has.

Will merging reduce the quality?

No. Pages are copied exactly as they are, so text stays sharp and selectable.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every Kindkit tool is free with no limits, no watermarks and no sign-up. There is no paid version.
